# AvalonBay Communities (NYSE: AVB) – Equity Valuation

Equity valuation of AvalonBay Communities, a US residential REIT with 314 apartment
communities across 11 states and Washington, DC. Built in Excel from five years of
historical financials (2020–2024), with a full written equity research report.

## Methods

**Discounted cash flow (DCF)**
- 5-year forecast (2025–2029) of unlevered free cash flow: EBIT × (1 – tax) less net CapEx and change in working capital
- Historical base: 6.58% average revenue growth and 61.17% EBITDA margin (2020–2024)
- WACC of 5.37%: cost of equity 8.18% (CAPM, beta re-levered from peer median at a 44.8% debt / 55.2% equity target structure), after-tax cost of debt 1.92%
- Terminal value by perpetuity growth at 2.8%; implied exit multiple of 18.7x EV/EBITDA against a peer median of 17.4x
- Sensitivity analysis across WACC (4.50%–6.00%) and terminal growth (1.50%–3.00%)

**Comparable company analysis**
- Peers: Equity Residential (EQR), Camden Property Trust (CPT), Essex Property Trust (ESS), Mid-America Apartment Communities (MAA)
- Multiples: EV/EBITDA (median 17.4x), EV/Revenue (median 10.7x), P/E (median 23.8x)
